# AIBrix Community Code of Conduct

AIBrix NeoCloud Node follows the
[CNCF Code of Conduct](https://github.com/cncf/foundation/blob/main/code-of-conduct.md).

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to `steering@aibrix.ai`. Reports are handled by
the AIBrix Steering Committee and kept confidential to the extent possible.

Members of the Steering Committee who do not follow or enforce the code in good
faith may be reported to the CNCF at `conduct@cncf.io`.
